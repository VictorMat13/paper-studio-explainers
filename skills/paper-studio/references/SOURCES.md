# Sources and what is not in this repo

Paper Studio was built by studying other people's work. Their files are not redistributed here; the analysis is.

- **The reference short:** InsiderForce, the JCode short on YouTube (video id `enFgYQvI1dM`, https://www.youtube.com/shorts/enFgYQvI1dM). `reference-breakdown.md` describes it shot by shot in words. The upscaled frame strips that the original skill kept beside it (`strip1.jpg`, `strip2.jpg`) are frames from that video, so they are left out. Watch the short yourself, and watch our own finished videos in `reference-videos/` at the repo root.
- **Moodboard:** `pinterest-moodboard/links.md` lists the Pinterest images with a note on what each one taught us. The images themselves are left out.
- **The ep08 reference reel:** a creator's Instagram reel on the same topic, used for the order of ideas only, never its footage or look. Not included.
- **Logos:** official marks from each company's own press or brand pages, in `assets/logos/` and `public/paperstudio/logos/`. They belong to their owners.
- **Photo cut-outs** (hands, robot, laptop, scissors, camera and the rest in `assets/photos/` and `public/`): generated for this project, original, no brand marks.
- **Page captures** in `public/paperstudio/shots/` and `public/paperstudio/jcode/`: screenshots of public pages and of the open source JCode tool, taken on the build date. Prices change, so capture your own at build time.
