## Rules

- Write only your own files: `C/src/ptp08/beats/<Group>.tsx`, `C/src/ptp08/beats/<Group>/` for helpers, `C/public/ptp08/<Group>/` for assets you copy, `E/dispatch/PLAN-<YOU>.md`, `E/dispatch/COMPLETION-<YOU>.md`, stills under `E/dispatch/stills/<you>-*`, previews under `E/dispatch/previews/<you>-*`. Never edit the kit, the shell, `timing.ts`, `timeline.*` or anyone else's files. If the kit lacks something (the word chip, the paper tag with text, the clock chip, the magnifier, the person chip, the step strip, the branch wire with a merge curve), build it as a local helper in your folder and list it.
- **Shared looks, one owner each:** BA owns `C/src/ptp08/beats/Shared/` and ships there, in this order and early: `cta.tsx` (the CTA scene, COMMIT bubble, IG and YT variants, the guide page turn; copy ep07's `BodyA/cta.tsx` as the base and change the keyword, the guide file and the promise caption) and `wordChip.tsx` (the white word chip). BB and BC import both from `Shared/` (never copy them); until they exist, build from the storyboard and the kit defaults and swap the import in before your final stills. The timeline wire + commit cards use the kit's `CommitStrip`/`Commit`/`WirePath`/`WireNode` so they match across bodies; the GitHub slab, Git chip, Claude chip use `LogoChip` with the same radii in every group (slab 180 px wide, chips r 44 unless the shot needs bigger, say so in your plan). The terminal card: `TerminalCard` with the same bezel in HB, BodyA, BodyB, BodyC; the body builder of BodyB owns the prompt-typing look (`BodyB/terminalPrompt.tsx`, exported) and the others import it once it exists.
- **Fill the safe box** (x 65-1015, y 269-1248; hooks y 460-1240). Cards 700-880 px wide, cut-outs and stacks big, nothing small in the top half, no big empty band. Victor's most repeated note.
- Every shot under 45 frames, three motion layers in every shot (primary action, secondary update, ambient drift), bezier easing, no springs, no CSS animation. **No empty or near-empty frame ever.** Every frame moving.
- Camera: hold, move, hold (14-24 frames, bezier); zoom on every reveal (max 1.5 body, 1.06-1.08 hooks), track anything that travels (`follow` with 3-6 frames lag), pull back for big-picture lines (not below 0.88); every hold drifts; captions in the `hud`; the group starts and ends at identity. Light motion blur only on fast camera moves, never on text.
- Text never overlapped or crossed by any object or by the next caption line (the old line is gone before the new one appears there). Physical actions must read as real (the scissors straddle the wire, open and close, the cut opens behind the tips; the eraser erases what it passes; a bar crosses the chip).
- No `CrtPass` in beats (the shell applies it once). Shadows down-right from one top-left light. Nothing glowing or neon, no line art, no stamps or labels narrating the visual, no typography effects. Paper look only; never the reference reel's dark look.
- On-screen numbers only from the storyboard list. Official logos only. No follow ask. No em or en dashes. No "Breaking".
- Type-check: `cd C && npx tsc --noEmit -p src/ptp08/tsconfig.json` (fix only errors in your own files; never leave yours broken, one broken import breaks every bundle).
- Stills only through `E/dispatch/ptp-stills.sh` (`VARIANT=<any of the 18>`, global frames; one render at a time through the shared lock; many agents share it, so 6-8 stills per round and patience). Compare each still to the storyboard bullet and the skill, fix, repeat. Hook builders: stills of all three hand-offs (hook last frame vs body frame 0 for A, B and C bodies, e.g. variants A, AB, AC for HA). Before finishing: one half-size silent preview of your whole group with `ptp-preview.sh`, viewed as a 2 fps contact sheet AND every 5th frame with the safe area cropped and enlarged, every caption swap and entrance/exit at every 2nd frame (the ep06 lesson: 2 fps thumbnails miss overlapped text and fake physical actions). List the frame ranges you checked.
- Finish with `E/dispatch/COMPLETION-<YOU>.md`: shots built, still and preview paths, local helpers, exports other builders import, the frame ranges checked, anything off or left for the fix round. Reply with a short summary.
